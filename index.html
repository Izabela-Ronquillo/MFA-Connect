Our History

A journey of growth, and service to the Bacoor community.

Marvelous Faith Academy of Bacoor was established in 2003 by a group of educators with the primary goal of providing accessible and quality education to families in Bacoor City, Cavite. Over the years, the academy grew its academic footprint from early childhood education into a full basic education provider covering preschool, elementary, and junior high school levels.

2003

Our Founding Year

Founded in Mambog IV, Bacoor, the school opened its doors as a private, non-sectarian institution focused on holistic child development.

Mission & Vision

Guided by faith, driven by excellence, committed to every child.

Our Mission

Committed to helping students reach their full potential, guiding them into lifelong learners, and fostering leaders who actively advocate for social building and development through multi-disciplinary programs.

Our Vision

Guided by the values of love for God, for country, for society, and for the environment, the institution nurtures well-equipped individuals capable of overcoming and excelling in a changing world.
